Every time you pick up a classic desk ruler in the US, UK, Canada, or Australia, one side stops at **12 inches** (1 foot) and the other stops at **30 centimeters**. Understanding the exact `2.54` conversion factor between them prevents sizing surprises when ordering frames, luggage, shelves, or shipping cartons internationally.

Open our [Inch Ruler](/inch-ruler) on a calibrated monitor to inspect each whole-inch milestone live at 1:1 scale.

## Why 12 Inches Does Not Equal 30 cm

Since 1959, the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia share the exact **International Inch**:

- `1 inch = 2.54 cm` (`25.4 mm`) **exact**
- `12 inches (1 foot) = 12 × 2.54 = 30.48 cm` (`304.8 mm`) **exact**

Because `30.48 cm` exceeds `30 cm` by **`4.8 mm`** (roughly the thickness of three stacked US pennies), the `12"` tick and the `30 cm` tick on a dual ruler **do not line up**. Notice that mismatch near the end of any dual-scale ruler: `30 cm` actually equals **`11.811 inches`**, just under `11 13/16"`.
