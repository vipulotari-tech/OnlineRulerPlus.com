Whether you are matching a metric bolt to an imperial drill bit, reading 3D printing tolerances, or sizing watch straps, converting **millimeters (mm)** to **fractions of an inch** trips up nearly everyone because `25.4` does not divide cleanly by `16`.

Use the lookup tables below—or read millimeters directly with our calibrated [MM Online Ruler](/mm-ruler) and compare against our [Inch Ruler](/inch-ruler).

## Exact Conversion Formula

- **Millimeters to Decimal Inches:** `inches = mm ÷ 25.4`
- **Millimeters to Nearest `1/16"`:** `sixteenths = round(mm ÷ 1.5875)`
- **Millimeters to Nearest `1/32"`:** `thirty_seconds = round(mm ÷ 0.79375)`

## Five Golden Metric-to-Inch Pairs Worth Memorizing

If you frequently work across US/imperial and UK/metric specifications, these five pairs match within **0.13 mm** or better:

1. **`4 mm ≈ 5/32"`** (`3.97 mm`)
2. **`8 mm ≈ 5/16"`** (`7.94 mm`)
3. **`16 mm ≈ 5/8"`** (`15.88 mm`)
4. **`19 mm ≈ 3/4"`** (`19.05 mm` — classic wrench crossover)
5. **`50 mm ≈ 1 31/32"`** (`50.01 mm` — virtually exact at 5 cm)
