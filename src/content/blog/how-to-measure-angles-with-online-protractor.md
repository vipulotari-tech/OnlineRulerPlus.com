While measuring linear inches or millimeters on screen requires calibrating display PPI, measuring **degrees** has a built-in geometric superpower: **angles do not change when you zoom in or change monitor size**. A `30°` corner stays `30°` whether displayed on a 6-inch phone or a 32-inch 4K screen.

Open the [Online Protractor](/protractor) right alongside this guide to test each step.

## Three Steps to Measure Any Angle on Screen or in a Photo

1. **Load your reference (optional):** Paste a screenshot (`Ctrl+V` / `Cmd+V`), drop a PDF snapshot or image into [Online Protractor](/protractor), or align directly over an open window/drawing.
2. **Pin the vertex cleanly:** Drag the center pivot onto the exact point where the two lines meet.
3. **Align both arms (`Ray A` and `Ray B`):** Pull the baseline arm along one straight edge as far out from the vertex as visible—longer arms minimize 1-pixel angular jitter—then drag the second arm onto the opposite edge.

## Reading Interior, Supplementary, and Miter Cuts

Every vertex produces useful paired readings:

| Angle Type | Formula | Practical Example | Common Use |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Acute / Interior (`θ`)** | Direct arm delta (`< 90°`) | `35°` | Geometry homework, drafting, roof triangles |
| **Obtuse (`θ`)** | `90° < θ < 180°` | `135°` | Bay window trim, hexagonal frames (`120°`) |
| **Supplementary (`180° - θ`)** | Opposite straight-line complement | `45°` supplement to `135°` | Crown molding & outside corner checks |
| **Saw Miter Setting** | `(180° - Corner°) ÷ 2` (or `90° - θ/2`) | `94°` drywall corner -> **`47°` half-angle (`43°` miter)** | Framing odd corners neatly |

## Three Precision Tricks for Digital Protractor Measurements

- **Extend arms along the longest clean line:** Moving an arm endpoint 200 pixels from the vertex cuts angular wobble by 4× compared to stopping 50 pixels away.
- **Correct camera tilt first on real-world photos:** Shooting a staircase stringer or countertop corner diagonally distorts `90°` corners into `82°` or `98°`. Always hold your lens perpendicular to the surface when snapping an angle photo.
- **Verify true horizontal/vertical baselines with [XY Quadrant Guides](/quadrant):** Need to check if a scanned blueprint or artwork border is slightly crooked? Drop an XY baseline first to confirm `0°` level.
