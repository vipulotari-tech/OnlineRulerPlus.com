Many users confuse the layout rulers inside Microsoft Office web apps with standalone screen measurement tools. Because **Word for the web (Word Online)** and **PowerPoint for the web (PowerPoint Online)** implement a streamlined ribbon compared to desktop Microsoft 365, the ruler setting sits in different places—or behaves differently altogether.

## How to View or Add a Ruler in Word Online

In Microsoft Word for the web, you can display the horizontal margin and indent ruler in three quick clicks:

1. Look at the top-right corner of the Word Online toolbar and make sure the document is in **Editing** mode (not *Viewing* or *Reviewing*).
2. Select the **View** tab in the ribbon.
3. Click the **Ruler** checkbox (or toggle) to turn the horizontal ruler on above your document page.

### Important Limitations in Word for the Web

- **Horizontal ruler only:** Word Online displays the horizontal ruler (margins, first-line indent, hanging indent, and tab stops). The vertical left-edge ruler remains a **Word Desktop** feature.
- **Changing inches vs. centimeters:** Measurement unit changes (inches, cm, mm) live inside desktop Word (`File > Options > Advanced > Display`) or your Microsoft account regional locale settings rather than a quick ribbon button in the browser.
- **No physical scale guarantee:** Zooming Word Online to 100% does **not** automatically make 1 inch on the document ruler equal 1 physical inch on your laptop screen unless your OS display scaling and screen PPI match. If you need true physical dimensions before printing, verify on our [calibrated Online Ruler](/).

## How to Show a Ruler in PowerPoint Online

Unlike desktop PowerPoint (`View > Show > Ruler`), **PowerPoint for the web does not expose the standard horizontal and vertical layout ruler**. Instead, Microsoft provides three practical alternatives depending on what you need to accomplish:

| Goal in PowerPoint Online | Built-in Web Option | Exact Steps |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Align shapes & text boxes evenly | **Smart Guides** | Drag any object on the canvas; red dashed alignment and equal-spacing guides appear automatically. |
| Measure slide proportions on screen | **XY Quadrant / Online Ruler** | Open [XY Quadrant Guides](/quadrant) or our [Print Scale Guide](/blog/how-to-print-actual-size-ruler-100-percent-scale) to check physical proportions. |
| Adjust exact guides & slide ruler | **Open in Desktop App** | Click **Editing > Open in Desktop App** (if desktop Office is licensed), then enable **View > Ruler**. |
| Sketch straight geometric lines | **Draw > Ruler** | Open the **Draw** tab in supported touch/pen views to rotate a digital straightedge on canvas. |

## Measuring Document Elements Outside Office

If you only opened Word Online or PowerPoint Online to check whether a flyer, badge, or label fits a physical envelope (`4 × 6 in`, `DL`, or `A5`), measuring inside a word-processor zoom box often misleads you.

Instead, export a screenshot or test image and measure ratios in [Photo Ruler](/photo-ruler), or verify screen dimensions directly on the [Online Ruler homepage](/). For a quick overview of Office and general ruler FAQs, you can also browse the dedicated [OnlineRulerPlus FAQ hub](/faq).
